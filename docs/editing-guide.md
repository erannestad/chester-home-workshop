# Editing the Chester Home Workshop website

You'll make all changes at **app.pagescms.org**. Sign in with the email link you were invited with. Every time you click **Save**, the website updates by itself in about a minute.

On any page of the website, **Edit** in the bottom corner (next to **Admin**) opens that product, post, or page in the editor.

## Add a product

1. Click **Products** → **Add an entry**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Name**: e.g. *Indigo Shibori Linen*
   - **Photos**: click to upload. The first photo is the main one. Each file must be JPEG, PNG, or WebP and under about 3 MB (see Photo tips).
   - **Category**: pick from the list
   - **Price ($)**: just the number, e.g. `28`. Leave blank if it's quoted or "price on request."
   - **Price note** (optional): *per yard*, *8 oz jar*, *Original, 9 × 12 in.*, *Quoted per piece*
   - **Availability**: Available · Made to order / by quote · Sold
   - **Short description**: a sentence or two
   - **Full description**: as much as you like
   - **Details**: starts with Dimensions, Material (*watercolor on paper, mounted on panel*), and Year. Fill in the blanks. Change or delete a row if the product is not a watercolor.
3. Click **Save**.

## Copy a product

Handy when a new item is a lot like an old one, like another color of the same linen.

1. Open the product you want to copy and click **Make a copy** at the top.
2. Type a name for the new one, e.g. *Rust Shibori Linen*, and click **Make a copy**. (If you leave it blank, it's called *Indigo Shibori Linen (copy)*.)
3. Wait about a minute, then go back to **Products**. The copy is in the list with everything filled in, including the photos.
4. Open the copy and change what's different: the photos, the price, the description.
5. Turn off **Hide from website** and click **Save**. Copies always start out hidden, so nobody sees one before it's ready.

## Mark something as sold

Open the product → set **Availability** to **Sold** → Save. It stays on the site, marked "Sold," and moves to the end of the list.

## Take something off the site

Open the product → turn on **Hide from website** → Save. It's kept, so you can turn it back on later.

## Feature on homepage

On a product, **Feature on homepage** puts it in the journal on the home page (full color, among the posts).

- **Feature on homepage**: turn on to show it, off to take it out.
- **Order on homepage**: **1** is earlier (closer to the top) and **5** is later. Products with the same number are listed A to Z.
- **Size on homepage**: Small, Medium or Large. Most stay Small. Medium shows a larger photo, and Large is the same as Medium except on very wide screens, where it gets even more room. On a phone it is always one column.
- **Show more photos on homepage**: off (the usual) shows just the first photo. Turn on to add up to four small photos underneath.

A hidden product never appears, even with this on. This does not change the shop.

## Write a journal post

The right side of the home page is the journal: photos and a line or two about what's going on in the workshop, newest first.

Click **Journal** → **Add an entry** → give it a title (e.g. *Two chairs back home*), pick the date, add photos, and write a sentence or two → Save. The first photo shows large and the next four appear small underneath. Visitors can click a post to open it wider and step through all its photos, in the order you added them.

**Width** is how big the post shows on a wide screen: Small (the usual size), Medium, or Large. Medium is twice as wide as Small. Large is the same as Medium, except on very wide screens, where it gets a little more room. On a phone every post is one column.

To take a post down without deleting it, turn on **Hide from website**.

## Change the order of products

Give products a **Sort order** number. Lower numbers come first. Products without a number come after, alphabetically.

## Add a category

Click **Categories** → **Add an entry** → give it a name and a short description → Save. It then appears in the product Category list and on the site.

Turn on **Feature on homepage** on a category to show it in the bottom right corner of the journal, linking to that category's shop page. If more than one is on, the one with the lowest Sort order is shown.

## Edit page text

- **Pages → Home page**: introduction, the heading above the journal, the sharing photo (not shown on the page; it's the picture that appears when someone shares a link to the site), and **Keep the first photo in color** (the first journal photo stays in color; the rest follow Journal photos)
- **Pages → About page**: your story and a photo
- **Pages → Contact page**: the welcome text, also shown under Contact on the home page

## Announcements, email, phone, Instagram, Facebook

**Site settings** has the business details. For **Facebook**, open the Facebook page in a browser, copy the address from the top of the window, and paste it in. Leave Instagram or Facebook blank and its link disappears from the site. It also has two handy fields:

- **Announcement bar**: a short message across the top of every page, e.g. *Fresh honey is in. Pickup Saturdays.* Clear it to hide the bar.
- **Ordering note**: shown under the button on every product page (pickup, shipping, timing).
- **Journal photos**: how journal photos look on the home page.
  - *Full color*: the photos as they are.
  - *Tint*: each post gets a rusty color (orangy yellow, brown, plum, or green).
  - *Grayscale* (the usual setting): every photo the same warm gray, with a print-like texture.
  - With Tint or Grayscale, hovering a post shows the original. Featured products are always in full color.
- **Divide the journal by**: where the journal draws a line between groups of posts.
  - *Year*: a line between years (*2025*).
  - *Season, by month*: Summer is June to August, Fall is September to November, Winter is December to February, Spring is March to May. Shows as *Fall 2026*; winter crosses New Year, so it shows as *Winter 2025–26*.
  - *Solstice* (the usual setting): a new line at each one, June 21 and December 21. Shows as *2026 Summer Solstice*. Posts from January to June 20 sit under the December before, e.g. *2025 Winter Solstice*.

## Photo tips

- Natural daylight, plain background, and the same spot each time keeps the shop looking consistent.
- Photos display as portrait rectangles (a bit taller than wide) in the shop, so frame with a little room around the item.
- Each photo must be under about **3 MB**, or the editor shows “Failed to upload file: 413”. Phone photos are often larger than that. Uploading several at once does not help.
- On a Mac, open the photo in Preview → File → Export… → JPEG, quality 70–80, longest side about 2000 px. That is plenty; the website resizes them. If **Resize for website** is in the right-click Quick Actions menu, that makes a `{name}-cms.jpg` next to the original, about 1 MB.
- If an upload is refused and the file is already small, it is probably HEIC. On iPhone: Settings → Camera → Formats → *Most Compatible*. Or share or export it as a JPEG first.

If something looks wrong after saving, wait a minute and refresh. If it still looks wrong, text Eric.
